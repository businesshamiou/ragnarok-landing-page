GOOD TO KNOW

Before your
first adventure.

A few things to know before you take the next step.

Is the game free to play?+

Yes. Steam lists it as free to play, with in-game purchases. Check the listing for the latest purchase information.

Where does the play button take me?+

The offer is intended for the Ragnarok: The New World page on Steam for PC. There you can review the game information and follow Steam’s instructions.

Can I continue on another device?+

The official description advertises synced progress between PC, phone and tablet. Check the access and compatibility conditions for each version.

Which languages are supported?+

The Steam listing reviewed includes English, Indonesian, Thai and Simplified Chinese. Check Steam for the latest language information.

Do I need a Gravity account?+

The Steam listing requires a Gravity account, created through the game launcher.

Is this the classic Ragnarok Online?+

Ragnarok: The New World is a distinct 3D experience in the Ragnarok universe. It is not presented here as a reproduction of the classic game.
